How to use Kitchen Chaos

Four simple steps. You can come back to this page any time from the top menu.

  1. 1 Choose your recipe

    Pick one of the two dishes: Fluffy Pancakes or Spaghetti Bolognese. Check the ingredients and equipment before you start.

  2. 2 Allow camera access

    The camera lets SmartSpectra see your facial expressions while you cook. It turns on only when you say so, and nothing is recorded or saved.

  3. 3 Follow the instructions

    Each step shows exactly what to do, the amounts you need, and a timer when you need to wait.

  4. 4 Control without touching

    Use your face instead of your fingers. The guide is always on screen, so there is nothing to remember.

    • 😊Smile Next step
    • 😲Surprise Repeat this step
    • 😐Neutral Stay on this step

    Hold the expression for about a second. Big buttons and keyboard keys always work too, so you never have to make a face you are not comfortable with.

Privacy and your camera

Why the camera is needed

Kitchen Chaos uses your camera so the Presage SmartSpectra SDK can read your facial expressions. A smile moves to the next step and a surprised look repeats the step, so you do not have to touch the screen with messy hands.

When the camera is on

Only after you agree on the camera screen and press Start. It turns off when you press Stop camera, leave cooking mode, or close the app. Your computer's camera light shows when it is on.

How SmartSpectra processes it

According to Presage's documentation, all SmartSpectra measurements are computed on your device. The SDK contacts Presage's server to check the API key and subscription.

What is stored or shared

Kitchen Chaos does not record, save or upload video, and keeps no history of your expressions. Expression scores are used for a moment to decide "next" or "repeat" and then thrown away. Only your music and sound settings are saved on this computer.

SDK telemetry

The SmartSpectra SDK can send Presage one anonymous summary per session (SDK version, platform, frame counts, error codes). It never includes video, measurement values or user identifiers. Kitchen Chaos switches this off by default. Read Presage's telemetry page.

Not a medical device

Kitchen Chaos makes no health or emotion claims. Expressions are used only as buttons.
